A NOTE FROM US

Helpful context.
Your call.

Little Lens is a small, hand-curated guide to common titles. Ages are recommendations, not guarantees, and a title's official rating can vary by country, edition, or platform.

We call out common themes to help you decide what fits your child. We don't have a verified guide for every title yet; when we don't, we'll say so rather than guess.

When there is no verified Greek/PEGI rating, Little Lens uses a title-specific recommendation when a source is available or a Little Lens estimate based on the choice itself. Estimates are not official ratings, and creator channels can vary from video to video.

You know your kid best. That's the lens that matters.
